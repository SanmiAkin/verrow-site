Privacy notice

How we handle your email address.

Last updated 29 September 2026

Verrow is not open to landlords yet. The only personal data this website collects is what you send us by email.

Who we are

MangoStack Ltd is the data controller. Registered in England and Wales, company number 17196444. Registered office: 23 Eston Grove, Sunderland, England, SR5 1QB. Contact: hello@mangostack.co.uk.

What we collect

When you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything you write.

Why we use it

To reply to you, and to write to you when Verrow opens if you have registered interest. The lawful basis is your consent. We do not use it for anything else.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your details. Our email provider stores messages on our behalf.

How long we keep it

Until Verrow opens and we have written to you, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email hello@mangostack.co.uk.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash, and those services receive your IP address when the page loads.

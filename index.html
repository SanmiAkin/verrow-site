How it works

Three steps, one property at a time.

Certificates in a folder and reminders in a calendar make it hard to see what is missing. Verrow puts each duty in one list, with the law beside it.

  1. 1

    Add a property

    Enter the postcode and tell us how the property is let.

  2. 2

    See what applies

    Verrow lists each duty that applies, the date each one falls due, and what is still outstanding. Every duty links to the words in the law it came from.

  3. 3

    Keep the evidence

    Add each certificate to the vault. Verrow holds it with a fingerprint and puts it in your evidence pack.

Questions landlords ask

Before you register.

Does Verrow cover Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland?

No. Verrow covers rental property in England only.

Is this legal advice?

No. Verrow shows what the law requires and the words in the law each duty comes from. For advice on your own circumstances, speak to a solicitor.

How do I know a duty is right?

Every duty traces to the words in the law it came from, and Verrow shows you those words.

What if Verrow does not hold my council's licensing data?

Verrow says so, and tells you to ask the council directly. It will not tell you a licence is not needed when it does not know.

What does it cost?

Starter, up to 3 properties, is £9 a month. Portfolio, up to 10, is £19. Unlimited is £29. All prices include VAT, and the £15 deep report is included for subscribers.
